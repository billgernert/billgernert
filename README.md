## Bill Gernert

Infrastructure engineer in the Denver area. Fifteen years running Windows and Linux infrastructure in higher education and county government, including twelve years of VMware Horizon VDI across three environments, one of them built from scratch, with automation and security as the center of the last five.

AutomationLab is my self-hosted platform. It provisions servers from a pull request, renews certificates and rotates secrets before they expire, restores backups every week to prove they work, and runs an unattended fleet of AI builders behind review gates I wrote. Kubernetes, Argo CD, Terraform, Vault, Jenkins, Proxmox, Active Directory and Entra, all in one estate, because that is what hybrid shops actually run.

Before the lab: grew a Jenkins platform from 20 scripts to 135 Python and PowerShell jobs, ran two Windows-heavy estates, one of about 1,000 VMs with more than 700 Windows servers and one of a few hundred servers, automated Horizon image updates and pool management with PowerCLI and the Horizon Helper module for 350 desktops, and removed over 200 unused service accounts and put the rest on rotation. The Horizon scripts are in the production-automation repository.

The repositories here are the pieces that stand on their own. The full work history is on LinkedIn.
