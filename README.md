## Bill Gernert

Infrastructure engineer in the Denver area. Fifteen years running Windows and Linux infrastructure in higher education and county government; in 2026 I moved to platform engineering on purpose.

AutomationLab is my self-hosted platform. It provisions servers from a pull request, renews certificates and rotates secrets before they expire, restores backups every week to prove they work, and runs an unattended fleet of AI builders behind review gates I wrote. Kubernetes, Argo CD, Terraform, Vault, Jenkins, Proxmox, Active Directory and Entra, all in one estate, because that is what hybrid shops actually run.

Before the lab: grew a Jenkins platform from 20 scripts to 135 Python and PowerShell jobs, ran two Windows-heavy estates, one of about 1,000 VMs with more than 700 Windows servers and one of a few hundred servers, operated a 600 user Horizon environment, removed over 200 unused service accounts and put the rest on rotation, and wrote the PowerShell monitoring that meant we never had to buy a $30,000 a year product.

The repositories here are the pieces that stand on their own. The full work history is on LinkedIn.
